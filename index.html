READ THE DATA WITH CONTEXT

Concentrations are signals.
They are not the whole story.

Counts are not risk

These records do not include traffic volumes, walking or cycling exposure. More crashes may reflect more trips, not higher individual risk.

Not every crash is reported

Minor crashes may be underreported. Police records cannot describe all crashes or near misses, and reporting can vary between groups.

Hospitalization is estimated

The source estimates people transported to hospital. This is not a verified admission count or a measure of injury severity.

Association is not causation

Observational records can reveal patterns. They cannot establish that street design, a road-user group, or a time of day caused a crash.

A partial year needs care

2026 is incomplete. Compare equivalent observation periods before drawing trend conclusions; this explorer does not annualize the data.

Locations need validation

Intersection names may require further normalization. Missing street pairs are omitted only from intersection rankings; estimated and missing neighborhoods limit geographic comparisons.

TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

Sources & methodology

One record, one reported crash

This explorer reads the provided CSV snapshot of the Cambridge Police Department CPD Crash Log. Cambridge describes the live dataset as an automated extract of approved crash reports, updated on weekdays. This local snapshot does not update automatically.

Dataset quality checks will appear when loading completes.

Dates are parsed as Cambridge local wall-clock time. Counts are converted to nonnegative integers; blank or invalid numeric fields remain unknown. Unknown values do not satisfy injury, hospitalization, or mode filters. Estimated hospitalizations sum known values.

Cleaning rules & metric definitions

Street names are trimmed, case and spacing standardized, periods removed, and common street-type abbreviations expanded. Street pairs are sorted so reversed pairs group together. No fuzzy matching or geocoding is used; incomplete pairs are not treated as intersections. Exact duplicate rows are reported and retained because the extract has no unique crash identifier.

Injury crashes have at least one recorded injured individual. Walk / bike crashes involve at least one cyclist or pedestrian, counted once even when both occur. Mode series overlap: a motorist–cyclist crash appears in both series. Records with no recorded road-user mode remain in the all-mode total. All active filters are combined with AND; date endpoints are inclusive.

Findings and recommendations are recalculated from the selected records. Rankings use counts, with alphabetic tie-breaking. Hour findings report ties explicitly. The default view includes all available dates; the final year is marked partial. Incomplete selected calendar years are also marked partial in the trend.
